# Services

Engagements are scoped around the business problem, production constraints, and evidence required to verify the result. Fixed pricing is not listed because scope varies.

| Service | Suitable client | Typical problem | Deliverables | Relevant examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-Performance Business Websites | Established business or new product team | The site is slow, difficult to maintain, or unclear on mobile | Architecture, responsive implementation, performance work, deployment, release verification | PeterhofApart, ELVN |
| B2B Catalogs and Procurement Platforms | Distributor, manufacturer, or sourcing team | Large product data is difficult to navigate, quote, and expose to search | Catalog architecture, search, manufacturer/category navigation, RFQ and BOM workflows | ChipFasteners, ICPROM |
| SaaS and MVP Development | Founder or product team | A product needs to move from validated scope to a production-ready web application | Product architecture, full-stack implementation, validation, tests, deployment | ELVN, ARMSENS Academy |
| Product Architecture and Technical Consulting | Founder or engineering team | Product requirements, system boundaries, or delivery risks are unclear | Architecture review, implementation plan, technical priorities, and release strategy | ChipFasteners, ELVN, ARMSENS Academy |
| Technical SEO and Crawl Optimization | Content-rich or catalog business | Important routes are not consistently indexable or crawl cost is uncontrolled | Canonical design, metadata, structured data, sitemaps, crawl and migration checks | ChipFasteners, ICPROM, ELVN |
| Performance and Core Web Vitals Engineering | Product with slow real-user journeys | Rendering, payload, or backend work delays key pages | Measurement plan, runtime profiling, image and request-path optimization, verification report | ChipFasteners, ICPROM, PeterhofApart |
| Production Deployment and Infrastructure | Team without a repeatable release process | Releases are fragile and rollback is unclear | Docker/Nginx setup, TLS, health checks, logging, release and rollback runbooks | ELVN, ChipFasteners |
| Website Audits and Recovery | Business with regressions or an unstable production site | Performance, crawlability, UX, or deployment behavior has degraded | Evidence inventory, prioritized findings, controlled fixes, before/after verification | ChipFasteners, ICPROM |
| Multilingual Web Products | International product or learning platform | Locale structure and interaction behavior diverge across languages | Locale architecture, responsive content, language QA, metadata and route strategy | ARMSENS Academy |
| Custom Dashboards and Internal Tools | Operations or product team | Manual workflows and fragmented data slow decisions | Workflow mapping, typed interfaces, validation, role-aware UI, deployment | Available as a custom engagement |
